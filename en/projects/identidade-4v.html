Overview

The visual identity of 4V Comunicação was created to present the agency in a professional, recognizable and consistent way across its different points of contact with the public.

The Challenge

The main challenge was to create an identity capable of conveying creativity, trust and versatility while maintaining a simple visual language adaptable to both digital and print materials.

Solutions

  • Development of the brand's visual concept.
  • Logo creation and refinement.
  • Definition of the brand color palette.
  • Typography selection and organization.
  • Creation of complementary graphic elements.
  • Application of the identity across digital and print materials.
  • Standardization of the agency's visual communication.
Visual System

Identity Development & Applications

The identity was developed as a consistent visual system, combining the brand, colors, typography and graphic elements adaptable to different formats and points of contact.

Technologies & Skills

Branding Visual Identity Graphic Design Art Direction Adobe Illustrator Adobe Photoshop

Key Learnings

During development, I deepened my knowledge of brand building, visual system organization, graphic consistency and adapting visual identities across different formats and applications.
