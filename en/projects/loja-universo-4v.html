Overview

Loja Universo 4V was developed to organize the presentation of products in a digital environment, using consistent visual materials and clear communication.

The Challenge

The main challenge was to create a visual presentation that highlighted the products without exaggeration, while maintaining consistency across materials, clear information and adaptability across different digital channels.

Solutions

  • Creation of the store's visual identity.
  • Development of promotional banners.
  • Standardization of product images.
  • Creation of social media materials.
  • Visual organization of commercial information.
  • Development of materials for digital campaigns.
  • Adaptation of materials for different formats.

Shopping Experience Structure

The store was organized to present products clearly, facilitate navigation between categories and guide visitors through the main stages of the shopping experience.

  • Visual organization of products and categories.
  • Creation of a clear hierarchy for titles, prices and descriptions.
  • Use of calls to action at strategic points.
  • Standardization of product images and information.
  • Adaptation of the interface for desktop and mobile devices.
  • Development of a consistent visual identity for the store.

Development Process

The project began with defining the store structure and organizing the information that needed to appear on each page. Next, the visual elements, product copy and interface components were developed to maintain a consistent navigation experience.

  • Planning the page structure.
  • Organization of categories and products.
  • Creation and review of product descriptions.
  • Development of banners and promotional elements.
  • Configuration of interface components.
  • Navigation and responsive design testing.
