Overview

Revista NaMídia was developed as an editorial project focused on presenting articles, interviews, information and advertising content in an organized and visually consistent publication.

The Challenge

The main challenge was to distribute different types of content within a balanced editorial structure while ensuring readability, clear information hierarchy and visual consistency across the pages.

Solutions

  • Creation of a grid system to organize the pages.
  • Definition of hierarchy between headings, text and images.
  • Visual standardization of editorial sections.
  • Image treatment and framing.
  • Balanced integration of editorial content and advertisements.
  • Preparation of files for print production.

Editorial Organization

The project was structured to organize different types of content within a consistent editorial language. The distribution of text, images, headings and graphic elements was planned to improve readability and highlight each article.

  • Definition of the visual structure of the pages.
  • Creation of hierarchy for headings, subheadings and body text.
  • Balanced organization of text and images.
  • Development of pages with varied compositions.
  • Standardization of margins, alignment and spacing.
  • Maintenance of visual consistency across different articles.

Layout Process

The layout process began with an analysis of the available content and an assessment of the relative importance of each piece of information. From there, compositions were developed to preserve the publication's identity while maintaining readability, visual rhythm and clarity.

  • Analysis and organization of editorial content.
  • Definition of grids and composition areas.
  • Selection of the typographic hierarchy.
  • Image treatment and positioning.
  • Review of readability and visual balance.
  • Preparation of pages for presentation and publication.
