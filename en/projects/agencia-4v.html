Overview

The project was developed to present Agência 4V Comunicação professionally, organizing its services, positioning and visual identity into a clear and straightforward digital experience.

The Challenge

The main challenge was to organize the agency's services, positioning and institutional information into clear navigation while maintaining consistency with its visual identity and making it easier for potential clients to get in touch.

Solutions

  • Clear organization of the services offered.
  • Visual hierarchy designed to improve readability.
  • Consistent application of the agency's visual identity.
  • Responsive layout for different screen sizes.
  • Strategically positioned contact calls to action.
